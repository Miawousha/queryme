GrammairePT is an educational SvelteKit web app for teaching French grammar to children aged 8 to 13. It combines a Photoshop-style training mode — pupils pick a grammar tool and click words in a pre-tagged text — with a quest mode where pupils fight grammatical monsters by correctly identifying word natures and syntactic functions. Built on a custom SyMark markup that encodes word natures, syntactic functions, and grammatical groups.
