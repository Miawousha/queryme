travelbook is an internal web app at ION-Altergo for planning on-site engineering trips, tracking expenses, and generating customer reports. Built with Next.js 15, TypeScript, and shadcn/ui on a Linear-inspired surface; Google OAuth gates the team-scoped views and multi-currency expense tracking. Public org repo used by the engineering team to coordinate field work.
