## Context
Altergo is a battery intelligence cloud platform ([altergo.io](https://altergo.io)). It originated as **Edison Analytics** inside ION Energy, was renamed Altergo in 2019, and was established as its own company in 2021. When ION's hardware business was sold to Endurance Technologies, Altergo was retained as the standalone software business. Alexandre co-founded Altergo and moved to it **full-time in March 2025**, after his tenure as CTO of Maxwell Energy Systems.

## Role
Co-founder and CTO, leading a focused team — deliberately far smaller than the hardware-heavy organisations at ION Energy and Maxwell, reflecting Altergo's pure-software model. Co-founded with Akhil Aryan (CEO).

## What Altergo does
Altergo applies data science and machine learning to battery data so that owners and operators of battery fleets can monitor, predict, and extend the life of their assets after deployment:

- **Digital-twin modelling** — an ontological representation of cells, packs, sites, and sensors, with dynamic simulation underneath.
- **Battery intelligence IP** — a library of **44 proprietary models and functions** covering state of health (SoH), state of function (SoF), degradation, thermal, and failure-risk modelling, validated across chemistries.
- **Applications layer** — BESS sizing and solution design, predictive maintenance, augmentation planning, warranty management, revenue optimisation, and fleet dashboards.
- **Integrations** — SDK and APIs for EMS and SCADA systems, so the analytics layer plugs into existing operational stacks.

Roughly **700 MWh** of battery capacity is under management on the platform, with publicly cited outcomes including ~15% asset-life extension, recovery of 1.5%+ usable capacity, and prevention of a majority of early-life BESS failures.

## Customers
- **esVolta** — supplied the battery intelligence platform across esVolta's full portfolio of utility-scale battery storage projects in North America, originally committed at **581 MWh** when first announced (the pilot 8 MWh site in Irvine simulated savings of around $450k/year).
- **RePower** — supplied the battery intelligence platform.
- **Adani** — worked with Adani on gigawatt-hour-scale energy storage projects.
- Additional deployments visible in industry trackers include **Volvo**, **Wamtechnik**, and **Zenobē**.
