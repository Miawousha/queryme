## Context
Altergo is a battery intelligence cloud platform ([altergo.io](https://altergo.io)). It originated as **Edison Analytics** inside ION Energy, was renamed Altergo in 2019, and was established as its own company in 2021. When ION's hardware business was sold to Endurance Technologies, Altergo was retained as the standalone software business. Alexandre co-founded Altergo and moved to it **full-time in March 2025**, after his tenure as CTO of Maxwell Energy Systems.

## Role
Co-founder and CTO, leading a focused team of around 12 people — deliberately far smaller than the hardware-heavy organisations at ION Energy and Maxwell, reflecting Altergo's pure-software model.

## What Altergo does
Altergo applies data science and machine learning to battery data so that owners and operators of battery fleets can monitor, predict, and extend the life of their assets after deployment:

- **Digital-twin modelling** of battery cells, packs, and fleets.
- **Analytics and machine learning** to estimate state of health, predict remaining life, and flag failure risk.
- A **cloud platform** that turns battery telemetry into operational decisions, improving fleet efficiency and return on investment across the battery life cycle.

## Customers
- **esVolta** — supplied the battery intelligence platform to esVolta, a utility-scale energy storage developer.
- **RePower** — supplied the battery intelligence platform.
- **Adani** — worked with Adani on gigawatt-hour-scale energy storage projects.

## Why this chapter matters
Altergo completes a 15-year arc from silicon to cloud. Alexandre started in power electronics and ASIC design, built hardware-led companies in Freemens and ION, and is now building the pure-software, data-driven layer of the same domain — the clearest demonstration of breadth across the full battery stack.
