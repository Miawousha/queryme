## Context
Freemens grew directly out of research at G2Elab (Grenoble INP / CNRS). Alexandre co-founded the company in 2011 with researcher **Jean-Christophe Crébier**, a specialist in power-electronics silicon integration. The founding idea was to bring power electronics on silicon *inside* the battery — managing energy flow between cells to extend life and improve safety, rather than treating the battery as a passive pack. Freemens was founded to commercialise that work.

## Role
Co-founder and CEO — co-founded Freemens with Jean-Christophe Crébier. Led the company from a two-person research spin-out to a commercial product company and through its acquisition. The team grew to nine engineers. Technical scope ran the full range — from ASIC design and power electronics through embedded firmware to cloud-based monitoring.

## What we built
- **FreeSafe** — an off-the-shelf battery management system (BMS) product family for high-power applications.
- **FreeWay** — a cloud platform for battery asset management and fleet monitoring.
- **BatLab** — battery emulation software used for BMS development and benchmarking.

Freemens served demanding, high-power use cases: electric vehicles, drones, speedboats, and electric racing. Customers included Airbus Safran, NTN-SNR, and CarWatt.

## Research
Led the **OPHELI** research programme in collaboration with G2Elab, studying battery aging mechanisms in relation to real in-situ application usage. The work connected laboratory aging science to how batteries actually behave in the field, which fed directly into the state-estimation and life-prediction logic in the products.

## Funding and recognition
- Early institutional backing: **INPG Entreprise SA** — represented by **Henri-Marc Michaud** — in 2012, followed by Pertinence Invest in 2013.
- **EDF Grand Prix "Innovative Energy"** — 2012.
- **H2020 iMoBatt** — won 2017, a European programme for modular battery industrialisation.

## Outcome
Freemens was acquired by **ION Energy** (Mumbai) in a cross-border cash-and-equity transaction announced in February 2018. The Freemens engineering and sales team and its technology moved to ION, and Alexandre joined ION as co-founder. Reporting at the time described it as a first-of-its-kind cross-border acquisition in the battery-management space.
