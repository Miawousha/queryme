## Context
Research engineer at G2Elab — the Grenoble Electrical Engineering Laboratory (Grenoble INP / CNRS) — working on power-electronics approaches to managing lithium-ion battery cells, alongside researcher **Jean-Christophe Crébier**, a specialist in power-electronics silicon integration.

## What I did
- Designed and developed a distributed, discrete active-balancing device, coupling an inverter topology with a next-to-next isolated converter.
- Designed and built a high-current, real-time battery emulator (hardware and software) for benchmarking active-balancing systems in a hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) environment.

## Why it matters
This research was the technical seed for everything that followed: it directly led to the founding of Freemens in 2011 — which Alexandre co-founded with Jean-Christophe Crébier — and the emulation and balancing work fed into the products built there.

## Publications
