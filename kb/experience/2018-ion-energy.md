## Context
ION Energy was founded in 2016 in Mumbai by Akhil Aryan, with the goal of improving the life and performance of lithium-ion batteries for electric vehicles and energy storage. In 2018 ION acquired **Freemens**, gaining close to a decade of battery-management engineering depth, a proven product line, and a base in France. Alexandre joined as co-founder and CTO.

## Role
Co-founder and CTO. Built and led an organisation that grew to roughly **70 engineers** spanning hardware, firmware, and software, operating across France and India. Owned the technology roadmap end to end, from BMS electronics to the cloud-analytics layer.

The organisation was structured as a set of specialised teams coordinated across two countries: three hardware teams, three embedded-systems teams, three test-and-validation teams, three application-engineering teams, and a dedicated systems and functional-safety (FuSa) team.

## What we built
- A battery technology platform covering BMS **hardware, firmware, and software**, built to accelerate customers' design, integration, and manufacturing and to support lasting field deployments.
- **Edison Analytics** — a battery intelligence and digital-twin platform that uses battery data, software analytics, and machine learning to remotely monitor, predict, and extend battery life. Renamed **Altergo** in 2019.
- Transferred Freemens technology into ION and built up remote engineering teams to maintain, extend, and develop new battery-management products.
- **Internal engineering infrastructure** — a custom system-engineering and requirements-management platform, and in-house hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) platforms for BMS test and validation.

## Customers and partnerships
- **Hero MotoCorp (HMCL)** — ION became the sole BMS supplier to Hero MotoCorp, the world's largest two-wheeler manufacturer. The programme delivered India's first functionally safe two-wheeler BMS, and later scaled into volume production under Maxwell Energy Systems.
- **Ola Electric** — collaborated closely on battery-management systems for Ola's electric two-wheelers, working directly with Ola's engineering leadership.
- **Technology licensing in Europe** — licensed battery-management technology to partners in Europe, including in France and Poland. The Polish partnership was with battery-pack manufacturer Wamtechnik, working closely with its R&D leadership; this relationship continued through the Maxwell phase.

## Scale and traction
- A cross-border France / India engineering organisation that grew to around 70 people.
- ION became a leading BMS supplier in India's two- and three-wheeler EV segment, with customers across roughly 12–15 countries.
- **Funding:** a $3.6M pre-Series A round in 2021, with participation from Amazon's Climate Pledge Fund alongside YourNest Venture Capital, Riso Capital, and Venture Catalysts.

## Notable collaborators
- **Roger Looney** — Head of Vehicle Engineering, Ola Electric (previously VP of Vehicle Engineering at Qoros Auto). Collaboration on battery-management systems for Ola's electric two-wheelers.
- **Jakub Zręda** — R&D, Wamtechnik. Battery-pack technology partnership in Poland.

## Outcome
ION's advanced-electronics and BMS hardware business was organised as **Maxwell Energy Systems** and acquired by **Endurance Technologies** for $40M in 2022. The **Altergo** software business was retained as a separate company. Alexandre moved on to lead technology at Maxwell through the acquisition.
